What this system cannot do

  • Sentinel satellites revisit every few days: no early warning, and the "after" image may be days old.
  • Radar in steep valleys suffers layover/shadow; these areas are masked, so flooding there is NOT assessed.
  • "Hit" means overlap with the detected extent, not structural damage. Small objects and flows narrower than ~20 m may be missed.
  • Cut-off analysis uses pre-event OSM roads: unmapped roads/trails, helicopter or foot access, and landslide blockages are not modelled.
  • Debris detection is heuristic (backscatter rise next to water) and untested outside the validation set.
